Cratewick · Privacy

Privacy,
clearly explained.

Cratewick stores game progress and settings on your device. Purchases are processed by Apple; advertising and consent services are provided by Google. Read the full policy for information use, privacy choices and support requests.

Your choices and questions

Advertising privacy choices

In the game, open Settings and use Privacy Choices when it is available. The options depend on your region and applicable consent requirements.

Apple purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple. For purchase and restore questions, see our support page.
