Row Counter: Knit Projects

Örgü Sayacı: Sıra ve Tığ

Keep your place in knitting and crochet projects with a row counter, notes and undo.

Örgü ve tığ işi projelerinde sıra sayacı, notlar ve geri alma ile kaldığın yeri koru.

EN

What you can do

  • Count rows in a named project and undo an extra tap.
  • Add notes and review a session summary.
  • Return to the saved count when you open the app again.

Free: Use one active project with the main counter, notes, undo and basic data export.

Optional Pro: A one-time purchase removes ads and unlocks multiple active projects, helper counters and repeat rules.

Project records stay on your device. The app does not automatically sync projects across devices. Core features work offline without an account. Ads and purchases may need a connection.
